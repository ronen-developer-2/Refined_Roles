# Privacy Policy for Refined Roles

**Effective Date:** 2026-02-04

**Refined Roles** ("we", "us", or "our") provides this Privacy Policy to inform you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Chrome Extension.

By using the Extension, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### a. User Data (Local Storage & Cloud Sync)
We do **not** collect, transmit, or store any of your personal browsing data, LinkedIn usage history, or search queries on our servers.
The Extension uses your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) to save your preferences. If you enable the "Cloud Sync" feature, your settings and blocklists will be synced securely across your Chrome devices using your Google account (`chrome.storage.sync`).

Data stored includes:
*   Keywords you wish to filter.
*   Company names you wish to block.
*   Interface settings (e.g., Ghost Mode toggle, Opacity levels, Whitelist Mode).

This data never leaves your device or your personal Google account. It is not accessible to us.

## 2. Permissions

The Extension requires the following permissions to function:
*   **Storage**: To save your filter preferences locally.
*   **Host Permissions (`LinkedIn`)**: To read the LinkedIn Job Search page content and modify the visibility of specific job cards based on your filters. We do **not** scrape, harvest, or sell any data from LinkedIn.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We maintain no servers of our own. No third-party services are communicated with.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
