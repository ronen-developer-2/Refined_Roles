# Privacy Policy for LinkedIn Job Cleaner

**Effective Date:** 2026-02-04

**LinkedIn Job Cleaner** ("we", "us", or "our") provides this Privacy Policy to inform you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Chrome Extension.

By using the Extension, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### a. User Data (Local Storage)
We do **not** collect, transmit, or store any of your personal browsing data, LinkedIn usage history, or search queries on our servers.
The Extension uses your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) to save your preferences, including:
*   Keywords you wish to filter.
*   Company names you wish to block.
*   Interface settings (e.g., Ghost Mode toggle, Opacity levels).
*   License validation status.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

### b. License Verification (Pro Users)
If you purchase a "Pro" license, you will be asked to enter a License Key.
*   The Extension sends this key to **Gumroad** (our payment and licensing provider) via their API (`https://api.gumroad.com`) to verify its validity.
*   We do not store your payment information, credit card details, or physical address. This information is handled entirely by Gumroad.
*   Please refer to [Gumroad's Privacy Policy](https://gumroad.com/privacy) for details on how they handle your data.

## 2. Permissions

The Extension requires the following permissions to function:
*   **Storage**: To save your filter preferences locally.
*   **Host Permissions (`LinkedIn`)**: To read the LinkedIn Job Search page content and modify the visibility of specific job cards based on your filters. We do **not** scrape, harvest, or sell any data from LinkedIn.
*   **Host Permissions (`Gumroad`)**: Strictly used for the purpose of validating your license key.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We maintain no servers of our own. The only third-party service communicated with is Gumroad (for license verification).

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
